About Thread Art

Thread Art (String Art) is a creative handmade art technique where threads are wrapped between nails or pins fixed on a wooden board to create portraits, names, logos, geometric designs, flowers and customized patterns.

Main Materials

🪵 Wooden/MDF Board
📌 Nails or Pins
🧵 Cotton/Nylon Thread
🔨 Small Hammer
✂️ Scissors
📐 Design Template

How It Works

A design is planned on the board, nails are placed around required points, and threads are connected repeatedly to create detailed images with curves, shading and beautiful patterns.
